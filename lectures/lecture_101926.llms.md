## Mass Percent

The mass fraction for each element can be calculated as:

\\ \text{Mass Fraction} = \frac{\text{Mass of element present in 1 mol}}{\text{Mass of 1 mol of compound}} \\

The mass fraction is then converted to mass by multiplying by 100%.

Example:

Ethanol has the formula \\\mathrm{C_2H_5OH}\\

\$\$ = \* 100 %

\*100=52.14 % \$\$

And so on for the other elements present as well.

## Empirical and Molecular Formula

The formula of a compound that expresses the smallest whole number ratio of the atoms present is called the empirical formula or simplest formula. - A compound that contains \\\mathrm{C_4H_8O_4}\\ has the same empirical formula as a compound that contain \\\mathrm{C_6H\_{12}O_6}\\. They both have the empirical formula \\\mathrm{CH_2O}\\.

The actual formula of a compound is the one that gives the composition of the molecules that are present it is called the molecular formula.
