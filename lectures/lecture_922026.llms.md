## Physical Changes

A Physical change involves a change in one or more physical properties of a substance but no change in the composition of that substance. E.g. Water freezing.

## Chemical Change

A chemical change transforms a substance into one or more new substances.

- Also referred to as a **reaction**

E.g. Electrolysis of Water:

- Water decomposes to hydrogen and oxygen gases when you apply an electric current to water.

- Cooking an egg is technically a chemical change because when you cool down the egg to it’s original temperature it doesn’t return to it’s original physical state.

## Elements

Elements only contain one type of atom. They cannot be broken down in other substances by chemical means.

- Examples:
  - iron \\\mathrm{Fe}\\
  - aluminum \\\mathrm{Al}\\
  - oxygen \\\mathrm{O}\\
  - hydrogen \\\mathrm{H}\\

## Compounds

Compounds are substances that contain two or more kinds of atoms.

Examples:

- Water, \\\mathrm{H_2O}\\
- glucose, \\\mathrm{C_6H\_{12}O_6}\\

**A Molecule** contains atoms bound together in a particular way.

Di-atomic elements are naturally paired with one more of their own atom to be stable.

Stable diatomic elements:

\\ \mathrm{Cr_2}\mathrm{I_2}\mathrm{N_2}\mathrm{Cl_2}\mathrm{H_2}\mathrm{O_2}\mathrm{F_2} \\

## Mixtures

A mixture has a variable composition.

Air is a mixutre because it can be seperated into its various component gases.

Heterogenous mixtures can be visually seen to be seperate.

Homogenous mixtures components cannot be easily seen by their individual components.

### Homogeneous Mixtures

Has the same properties throughout.

- Table salt and water form a homogenous mixture.
- Homogeneous mixtures are also known as solutions.

### Heterogenous Mixtures

When you can visually see the difference in mixture. There are said to be different properties in different parts of the mixture.

- Sand and water form a heterogeneous mixture.

### Pure Substances

- A pure substance always has the same composition.
- Pure substances can also be compounds, e.g. a glass full of (water)\\\space \mathrm{H_20}\\

**Types of Pure Substances**

Puresubstances are either:

- Elements
- Compounds

Elements cannot be broken down chemically into simpler substances.

Compounds can be broken down chemically into elements.
